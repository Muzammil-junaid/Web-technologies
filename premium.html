Questions? We've got answers

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your plan whenever you like and keep Premium until the end of your billing period.

What is the difference between Free and Premium?

Free has ads and shuffle-only play on mobile. Premium removes ads, lets you play any song, and lets you download music.

Can I share Premium with my family?

Yes. The Duo and Family plans let several people listen with their own accounts.

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?

This is a demo page, so nothing is charged. In a real app, payment details would be required to start a trial.
